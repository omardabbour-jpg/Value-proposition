How to play — Mystery Widget Pitch Tank

Tap to expand the 6 rounds your team will run through

1

Draw a widget and an origin story

Press Generate below to get your team's product. Decide as a group whether your company is Product-Led (built it, then found buyers) or Market-Led (found the need, then built it) — your assigned widget's background will help you argue either way.

2

Fill in the 7 characteristics

Use the "rich in" tags on your widget card as a starting point, then complete Parts 2–4 of the template: 3 product characteristics, 3 service characteristics, 3 customer expectations.

3

Write the mission statement

One sentence, modeled on McDonald's: "Food of a constant quality that is served quickly and consistently across the globe." It should reflect both your product and the expectations you're setting.

4

Check Value, Need, and Trust

Before pitching, make sure Part 1 clearly answers: what VALUE does this solve, what NEED does it address, and why would a customer TRUST this brand over a competitor's version of the same object?

5

60-second pitch to the investor panel

Pitch your reinvented widget to classmates acting as investors. They can only ask questions tied to the 7 characteristics or the Value/Need/Trust triangle — no generic "I like it" feedback.

6

Panel scores and class debrief

Investors score whether you named a real need, built trust, hit all 7 characteristics, and landed a tight mission statement. Discuss as a class which origin story was most convincing, and why.
